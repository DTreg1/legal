# WorkSpAIce Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 20 May 2026_

WorkSpAIce ("we", "us", "the app") is an inventory and project-assistant tool for makers. This policy describes what we collect, what we do with it, and the controls you have. It is written in plain English on purpose — if anything here is unclear, contact us (see the bottom) and we'll clarify.

## 1. Who's running this

WorkSpAIce is operated by D. Trega ("we"). The app is distributed on the Apple App Store; the backend runs on a server we operate. We are not a data broker; we don't sell user data and we have no interest in doing so.

## 2. What we collect

When you use WorkSpAIce we store the minimum needed to run the app:

- **Account** — email address, a bcrypt-hashed password, and the display name you choose. We never see your plaintext password.
- **Inventory and tools** — the components, tools, organizers, printers, filament, resin and any other items you log. This includes any photos you attach.
- **Projects** — titles, descriptions, BOMs, build steps, and any printable STL or schematic files generated for them.
- **Subscriptions** — your current tier (free / BYOK / all-inclusive), the Apple transaction id linking your purchase, the auto-renew state. We do not see, store, or process your payment-card details; Apple handles billing entirely.
- **BYOK OpenAI key (optional)** — if you save your own OpenAI key in Account → OpenAI key, we encrypt it with AES-256-GCM at rest. Only the last four characters are ever returned by the API for display; the plaintext key is decrypted server-side per request and is never logged.
- **Helper-chat transcripts** — the user-visible messages you send to the AI helper and the helper's replies are processed in-memory during each turn; the conversation thread state is held by OpenAI via `previous_response_id` so we can continue a multi-turn conversation without re-sending the full history. We do not persist chat history on our servers.

We do NOT collect: location, contacts, IDFA / advertising id, biometric data, browsing history, or any third-party analytics beyond the high-level App Store Connect aggregate metrics Apple shows to every developer.

## 3. How we use it

Solely to run the app. Specifically:

- Authenticate you against your account.
- Show you your inventory, projects, etc.
- Send your user-visible chat messages to OpenAI (or to your own OpenAI account if you're on the BYOK tier) so the helper can respond.
- Compile OpenSCAD source into STL + preview PNG when you ask the helper to generate a 3D-printable part.
- Look up product details on Mouser Electronics when you paste a part number or URL into the add-component flow.
- Process App Store subscription events through Apple's App Store Server Notifications service so we can keep your tier accurate.

We do not use your data to train any AI model, including OpenAI's.

## 4. Third parties we share data with

For the operations above, the following third parties receive specific pieces of your data:

- **OpenAI** — your chat messages and the tool-call contexts (inventory excerpts, etc.) needed to respond. Subject to OpenAI's API terms; we use the standard API where prompts are not used for model training. If you use BYOK, your data goes to your own OpenAI account, not ours.
- **Cloudflare R2** — STL and preview PNG files generated by the helper are stored here so the app can download them. Files are scoped to your user id in the storage key path.
- **Mouser Electronics** — product part numbers / keywords you submit via the lookup feature.
- **Apple** — subscription state, transaction id, original transaction id, auto-renew flag (this is what App Store Server Notifications send us, in reverse).

Each of these is contracted as a processor, not a recipient — they process the data on our behalf, not for their own purposes.

## 5. How long we keep it

Indefinitely while your account exists, because the inventory and project data is the entire point of the app and we don't know which items you still care about.

When you delete your account (Account → Delete account in the app, or DELETE /api/auth/me via the API), the database `ON DELETE CASCADE` removes every row that referenced your user id across all 11 tables that store per-user data. STL and preview PNG files in R2 are not auto-deleted by the cascade today — operationally we run a periodic sweep, but if you want them removed sooner email us and we'll handle it manually.

OpenAI's previous-response thread state is retained by OpenAI on their own schedule (typically 30 days for the Responses API); we cannot control or delete that. Mouser does not receive any user-identifying data, so there's nothing to delete on their side.

## 6. Your controls

- **Delete account** — in the app, Account → Delete account. Wipes everything we store about you, server-side.
- **Remove your saved OpenAI key** — Account → OpenAI key → Remove key.
- **Export** — not available in-app today. Email us if you need a one-time export of your projects/inventory; we'll send a JSON dump within 14 days.
- **Cancel subscription** — via iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. We don't have access to your payment instrument; Apple handles cancellation and prorating.

## 7. Children

WorkSpAIce is rated 4+ but is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

All traffic between the app and our backend is TLS. Passwords are bcrypt hashed. BYOK OpenAI keys are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest. JWTs expire after 30 days. We have no employees other than the operator.

That said: no system is unbreachable. If we ever experience a breach that exposes user data, we will notify affected users within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

## 9. Changes to this policy

If we change anything material we'll update the "Last updated" date and notify you in-app the next time you open it. Non-material changes (typos, clarifications) may be made silently.
